Everything you need to know about our clinical safety, treatment recovery, pricing, and consultation preparations.

With dermal fillers, volumetric enhancement and contouring are visible immediately post-injection, with full settlement into tissues occurring within 7 to 10 days as mild swelling subsides. For laser therapy and scar resurfacing, collagen remodeling is a progressive cellular process: initial surface radiance and tone improvements are noticeable within 14 days, with maximum scar smoothing occurring over 2 to 3 months across completed sessions.

Yes, absolutely. Many older lasers were limited to fair skin, but Aura utilizes specialized dual-wavelength platforms (such as 1064nm Nd:YAG and picosecond technology) that bypass epidermal melanin to treat deep target tissue without triggering post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation (PIH). Every treatment is calibrated specifically to your individual Fitzpatrick classification.

For Hair Loss PRP and Hydra-Glow, there is zero downtime; you can immediately return to work, exercise, and social engagements. For Fractional Laser Resurfacing, patients typically experience 2 to 4 days of mild redness similar to a slight sunburn. We provide barrier repair balms and mineral sunscreens that allow normal desk duties the following day.
