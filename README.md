# Aura Dermatology & Aesthetic Institute - Premium Landing Page

A premium, medical-grade, modern healthcare and dermatology clinic landing page built with clean HTML5, CSS3, Bootstrap 5, and JavaScript. Designed for high conversion, responsive usability, and aesthetic trust.

## ✨ Features & Sections Included

1. **Sticky Navigation Bar**
   - Clinical emergency & working hours top bar
   - Clinic logo & branding mark
   - Smooth-scrolling navigation links with active scrollspy
   - Direct call CTA & "Book Appointment" button triggering interactive modal
   - Mobile-responsive collapsible navbar

2. **Hero Section**
   - High-resolution dermatology clinical imagery
   - Headline: *"Advanced Skin & Hair Care Solutions"*
   - Dual CTAs: *Book Consultation* & *Explore Treatments*
   - Three floating medical glassmorphic badges (`99.2% Success Rate`, `US-FDA Approved Tech`, `25+ Board Specialists`)
   - Social proof with overlapping patient avatars and star ratings (15,000+ patients)

3. **Services Section**
   - Interactive category filter pills (*All Services*, *Skin Rejuvenation*, *Laser & Scars*, *Anti-Aging*, *Hair & Scalp*)
   - 6 user-specified specialized service cards with zoom hover effects:
     - Stretch Marks & Scars (Fractional CO2 Laser & RF Microneedling)
     - Dermal Fillers (Juvederm® & Restylane® facial contouring)
     - Hair Loss & PRP (Autologous follicle biostimulation)
     - Acne Treatment (ClearSkin clinical protocol)
     - Laser Therapy (Picosecond & Nd:YAG pigment clearance)
     - Skin Rejuvenation (Hydra-Glow medical infusion)
   - Dynamic "View Details" modal for each procedure with duration, downtime, and clinical benefits

4. **Why Choose Us**
   - Two-column layout featuring Medical Director *Dr. Elena Vance, MD, FAAD*
   - 4 clinical pillars: Certified Specialists, Modern Equipment, Personalized Care, and Proven Results
   - Live animated statistics counter (`15,000+ Patients`, `25+ Specialists`, `98.6% Efficacy`, `12+ Awards`)

5. **Treatment Process**
   - 4-step structured roadmap:
     1. *Consultation* (1-on-1 doctor review)
     2. *Diagnosis* (3D multi-spectral dermascan)
     3. *Treatment* (Sterilized, pain-minimized suite)
     4. *Follow-up Care* (24/7 post-care monitoring)

6. **Interactive Before & After Comparison Slider**
   - Draggable split-view slider showcasing real patient skin rejuvenation results

7. **Patient Testimonials**
   - Verified patient reviews carousel with 5-star ratings, condition treated, and authentic feedback

8. **Appointment Booking CTA & Form**
   - Gradient section with glassmorphism styling
   - Form fields: Full Name, Phone, Email, Desired Treatment, Preferred Doctor, Date Picker, Time Slot, and Notes
   - Instant client-side validation, loading indicator, and toast feedback confirmation

9. **FAQ Section**
   - Bootstrap 5 animated accordion addressing recovery times, skin tone safety (Fitzpatrick I-VI), financing, and preparation

10. **Contact Section**
    - Address, direct phone, toll-free lines, email, and operating hours
    - Full Google Maps embed for Beverly Hills clinic location

11. **Footer**
    - Clinic mission, medical accreditations (AAD, FDA, HIPAA, Board Certified)
    - Quick navigation links, newsletter signup, social media buttons, and legal disclaimer

12. **Delight Features**
    - Floating WhatsApp quick chat button
    - Smooth scroll-to-top floating button
    - Toast feedback for forms and newsletter

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- **HTML5** (Semantic structure, SEO meta tags, OpenGraph)
- **CSS3** (CSS custom variables, Glassmorphism, CSS Grid & Flexbox, smooth transitions)
- **Bootstrap 5.3.3** (Responsive framework, carousel, modal, accordion, utilities)
- **Font Awesome 6.5.2** (Clean medical and navigation vector icons)
- **AOS (Animate on Scroll) 2.3.4** (Lightweight scroll animations)
- **Google Fonts** (*Plus Jakarta Sans* & *Inter*)
- **Vanilla JavaScript** (Zero heavy framework overhead, fast performance)

## 🚀 How to Run Locally

You can open `index.html` directly in any web browser or serve it using any simple local server:

### Option 1: Direct Browser Launch
Double click `index.html` or open via browser:
```bash
start index.html
```

### Option 2: Python Local Server
```bash
python -m http.server 8000
```
Then navigate to `http://localhost:8000` in your browser.
